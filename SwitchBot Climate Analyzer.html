How to break out of the Gemini app preview

Method 1: Gemini Public Link (Easiest) No Coding
  1. At the top or bottom of this Gemini screen, tap the Share icon (or 3-dots menu) and select "Share" → "Create public link".
  2. Copy that link, open your Chrome app, and paste it into the address bar.
  3. Once open in Chrome, tap Chrome's 3-dots menu (top-right) and tap "Add to Home screen"!
Method 2: Turn Into Your Own Website URL True Web App

Want a dedicated website URL that opens straight into the app without Gemini around it?

  1. Tap "1. Download .HTML File" above (or if the Gemini app blocks downloads, tap "Copy App Code").
  2. In Chrome, go to tiiny.host or app.netlify.com/drop (both free).
  3. Upload the index.html file (or paste the code). It will instantly give you your own https://... website link!
  4. Open that link in Chrome and tap 3-dots → Add to Home screen.
